Our Institutional Direction

Vision, Mission, Goals & Objectives

VISION

A 21st Century University transforming a global network of learners for enlightenment, leadership, and human service.

MISSION

Aldersgate College was established to develop the power of reason, to liberate people from ignorance, penury, and hopelessness.

GOALS & OBJECTIVES

1
Educate individuals to think and act as ethical leaders.
2
Inculcate critical and innovative thinking and technological fluency.
3
Deliver affordable, flexible, and collaborative educational programs.
4
Offer career pathways through a network of transnational institutions.
